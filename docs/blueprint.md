# **App Name**: BerryHaven

## Core Features:

- Product Showcase: A responsive landing page displaying all furniture products with high-quality images, descriptions, and pricing information in a clear grid layout.
- Secure Checkout with Paystack: Integration with Paystack for secure payment processing, allowing users to pay for selected products using physical or virtual cards through a modal dialogue.
- Direct WhatsApp Inquiry: A WhatsApp button on each product page that, when clicked, redirects the user to a pre-populated chat to easily inquire about the specific product.
- Informational Pages: Dedicated sections for 'About Us', 'Services', and 'Contact Us' to provide essential company information and ways for customers to reach out.
- AI Interior Style Assistant: A generative AI tool that recommends furniture pieces or decor combinations based on user input about their desired home aesthetic or room characteristics, enhancing the shopping experience.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A warm, sophisticated mid-brown (#AB7339), chosen to evoke natural wood tones, comfort, and an inviting atmosphere suitable for a furniture brand. (HSL: 30, 50%, 45%).
- Background color: A very light, desaturated cream (#F5F2F0), providing a clean, airy canvas that subtly carries the warmth of the primary hue, ensuring a 'smooth UI' feel. (HSL: 30, 15%, 95%).
- Accent color: A rich, deep red (#A32929), chosen to be analogous to the primary while providing strong contrast for calls to action and important highlights. (HSL: 0, 60%, 40%).
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif font for an elegant, high-end, and fashionable feel suitable for a furniture brand's visual identity.
- Body text font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif font, chosen for its readability, modern look, and touch of warmth, perfectly complementing the elegant headlines.
- Use clean, minimalist, line-based icons to maintain a sophisticated and modern aesthetic throughout the 'smooth UI'.
- Employ a grid-based, responsive layout with ample white space to ensure a luxurious feel and excellent readability across all devices. Product images should be large and engaging.
- Incorporate subtle hover animations on interactive elements like product cards and buttons, and smooth page transition effects to enhance the overall 'smooth UI' experience.
